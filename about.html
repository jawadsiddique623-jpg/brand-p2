NEED TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How long does shipping take?

Orders within Bangladesh generally arrive in 2–5 business days, depending on your location. Delivery timing will be confirmed when your order is placed.

What is your return policy?

Unused items in their original condition may be eligible for return or exchange within 7 days. Please contact our team before sending an item back.

How do I choose the right size?

Check the size information on each product page. If you are unsure, message us with your usual fit preference and measurements for guidance.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment options can be confirmed at checkout or by contacting our support team. Available methods may vary by delivery location.

How can I track my order?

After dispatch, contact our team with your order number to receive the latest delivery update.
